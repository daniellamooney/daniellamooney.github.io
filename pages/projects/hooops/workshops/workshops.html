Power Tools workshop / Berlin, 2026

This was a hands-on workshop where we used easy practical skills to build our own set of tools. Playing with the possibilities of useful new appendages, we explored ways in which manifesting objects into being can give us a sense of agency in moments of need. Simple methods of construction were learnt as well as hand-building techniques to craft objects of ones own design. During the session participans were guided to explore resourceful methods in order to expand the idea of what a tool can be - whether making tools of subversion, memory prosthetics, extensions of desire or even small modifications. By using what’s on hand, in hand, at hand, together we discovered how these crafted tools can become extensions of us, our stories and intentions.
