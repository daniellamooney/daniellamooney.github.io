Projects


Hooops is a research club, publisher and travelling community library for books on medicinal plants and mushrooms. They are a platform for sharing, exchanging, and storing knowledge about the potentials of local plants and substances that could be used in contexts of healing and therapy. They share books, knowledge and recipes.
I sometimes contribute to their magazine publications as well as host collaborative workshops.
