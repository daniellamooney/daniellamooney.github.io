Cabin / Potsdam NY / Fall 2026

This series of paintings was produced during a two month residency stay at Cabin over the Autumn of 2026. The paintings locate themselves within a fictionalised narrative developed over the first few weeks of living in Potsdam upstate New York. Chance encounters with locals, mineral histories, trilobites and flood warnings merge in a layered world where deep time and deep strata reveal new cycles and horrors. Becoming geological is a theme explored and often returned to in my paintings and sculpture, and this body of work allowed me to simultaneously world-build with local specificity and also speculate with what happens, when warnings in stone can no longer be deciphered.
